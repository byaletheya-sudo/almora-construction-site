B3Questions

Common questions

Can I build an ADU on my lot?

Most single-family lots in Los Angeles can have at least one ADU, and many can have a junior ADU too. The best way to know is a quick visit. It's free.

Do you handle the permits?

Yes. We prepare and submit the plans, answer the city's comments, and schedule every inspection.

How much does an ADU cost?

It depends on size, type and what's already on the lot (utilities, slope, access). After the visit you get a written estimate with no obligation.

Can I rent it out?

In most cases yes. Lots of owners build one for rental income, others for parents or grown kids.

Do I need to move out during the build?

For a detached ADU, no. The work happens in the backyard. We keep the site clean and tell you ahead of any noisy days.
